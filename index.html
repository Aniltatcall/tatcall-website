TatCall Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Phone contacts (stored locally only)
  • Phone number for registration
  • Call metadata (duration, time)

2. How We Use Information

  • To facilitate voice calls between users
  • To display contact names during calls
  • All data is stored on your device and Firebase

3. Data Sharing

  • We do NOT sell your data
  • We do NOT share with third parties
  • Calls are peer-to-peer encrypted

4. Permissions Used

  • RECORD_AUDIO: For voice calls
  • READ_CONTACTS: To show your contacts
  • CALL_PHONE: For emergency calls
  • INTERNET: For call signaling
  • CAMERA: For future video calling (not yet implemented)

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. All calls use WebRTC encryption.

6. Your Rights

  • You can delete your account anytime
  • You can request data deletion
  • You control your contact permissions

7. Contact Us

For privacy concerns, email: privacy@tatcall.app

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Check this page regularly for updates.
